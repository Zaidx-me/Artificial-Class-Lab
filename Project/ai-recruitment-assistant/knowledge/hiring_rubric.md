# Company Hiring Rubric (Software Engineering)

## Scoring weights (total 100)
- Technical skills match: 50 points. Award points in proportion to how many required skills in the job description are demonstrated with concrete evidence (projects, work history), not just listed.
- Relevant experience: 35 points. Roughly 7 points per year of directly relevant professional experience, capped at 35. Internships count as half. Academic projects count for at most 8 points.
- Education: 15 points. 15 for a completed relevant bachelor's or higher degree (CS, IT, SE, Engineering). 8 for a related degree or in-progress degree. 4 for unrelated degree with strong self-taught evidence.

## Recommendation bands
- 80-100: Strong hire - invite to technical interview.
- 60-79: Potential - schedule a phone screen.
- 40-59: Weak - keep on file, only if the pipeline is thin.
- below 40: Reject.

## Red flags
- Employment timeline gaps longer than 12 months with no explanation.
- Skills listed with no supporting project or job evidence.
- Claimed seniority inconsistent with years of experience.

## Fairness rules
- Never use name, gender, nationality, age, photo or university prestige as a scoring factor.
- Judge only on evidence in the resume relative to the job description.
- Always give a written justification for every score.

## Software engineer skills guidance
Core backend skills: Python, REST APIs, SQL databases, Git, automated testing. Valued extras: Docker, cloud platforms (AWS), CI/CD, Linux, caching (Redis), message queues.
Frontend or data-science-only candidates should be scored on transferable backend evidence only.
